It’s Giving Support

Need help with It’s Giving? We’re here to help.

My AI feature isn’t working

Please check that you have a stable internet connection and that you are using the latest version of It’s Giving.

If the problem continues, contact support and tell us your device model, operating system, which feature you were using, and what happened or what error appeared.

My subscription isn’t showing

If you purchased a subscription but your premium features are not active, first try using the Restore Purchases option in the app.

If that doesn’t work, contact support and include the email associated with your It’s Giving account.

Please do not send your full payment-card information.

I want to delete my account

You can request deletion of your It’s Giving account and associated personal information by contacting our support team. Include the email address associated with your account so we can locate it.

I have a problem with a photo or AI analysis

Tell us which feature you were using and what went wrong. If possible, include a screenshot of the problem.

Please do not send sensitive personal information that isn’t necessary to resolve the issue.
